## Art and audio

None yet. Every model, icon, and portrait in the game is generated in code from primitives.

Third-party assets are added only after the project owner has approved them and their licence has been checked on the creator's own page. Each one is then listed here with its creator, a link, its licence, and what it is used for. Even when a licence does not require attribution (for example CC0), the creator is credited here and on the title screen.
